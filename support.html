Support

Help with Heroes Overload.

Contact us

Write to heroesoverload.support@gmail.com. We answer in English or Italian, usually within two working days.

In the game, Stronghold › Profile › Settings › Report a problem opens an email with your game details already filled in. Please include your player ID (Settings › Account) so we can find your profile.

Purchases

Crystals are bought through the App Store. If crystals you paid for didn't arrive, close and reopen the game, or tap Settings › Restore purchases. If they still don't arrive, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your profile

Playing as a guest keeps your progress on this iPhone's account only. Sign in with Apple (Settings › Account) keeps it safe if you change phone.

To delete your profile and everything we keep about it, tap Settings › Delete profile. This can't be undone.
