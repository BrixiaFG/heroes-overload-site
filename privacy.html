Privacy Policy

What Heroes Overload keeps about you, and why.

Short version: we keep only what the game needs to work and to get better. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Heroes Overload is made by Brixia, who is responsible for your data. Contact: heroesoverload.support@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: a player ID made when you first open the game (as a guest), or your Sign in with Apple account. With Apple we receive an ID and, if you share it, an email address (often a private relay address from Apple). Your player name and friend code.
  • Your game: cards, Heroes, coins, crystals, Aether, packs, Campaign stages, Ranked points and history, your squads, Stronghold progress, gifts, and friends and blocks.
  • Purchases: what you bought and when, from Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
  • How you play: the days you play, visits and their length, which screens you open, fights left partway, tutorial steps, and reminder taps. We use these only to understand what to improve.
  • Your device: the app version, your language, and, if you allow notifications, the phone's push address.
  • Internet address: seen by our server host for a short time to keep the game safe (for example, limiting new guest accounts). We don't store it with your profile.

Why

To run the game and keep your progress (our contract with you), to stop cheating and abuse, and to improve the game (our legitimate interest). Notifications only with your permission, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your player name, Hero, league and your Defending squad. Friends also see your friend profile.
  • Supabase hosts our servers and database. RevenueCat checks App Store purchases. Apple runs Sign in with Apple, the App Store and notifications. They process data only for us.

No advertising networks, no third-party analytics, no data brokers.

How long

Your profile stays as long as you play. Records of how you play are deleted after 90 days. A guest profile that hasn't been used for a long time and never bought anything may be deleted. Purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings › Delete profile. This also ends Sign in with Apple for the game.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop using it, by writing to heroesoverload.support@gmail.com.
  • If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13. If you are under 16, please ask a parent before playing or buying.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. Big changes are also shown in the game.
